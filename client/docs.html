CAMO is a trading terminal for Robinhood Chain tokens where every position sits in its own wallet and no wallet can be followed back to the one that funded it.

Getting started

  1. Open the terminal and press Open vault. Choose a passphrase and save the recovery key it shows you.
  2. Open Compartments and press Fund Reserve. Pick the asset and chain you are paying from, enter the amount and a refund address, and send to the one-time deposit address. ETH arrives in Reserve on Robinhood Chain, paid by the route.
  3. Find a token in Discover or paste its contract address into search.
  4. Press Buy. With "New compartment (isolated)" selected, the terminal derives a fresh wallet, pays it through the route and buys from it.
  5. To exit, sell from the token page, then use Return to Reserve on the compartment and Burn it.

Vault

The vault is a 32-byte seed generated in your tab with the browser's secure random source. It is encrypted with a key stretched from your passphrase (PBKDF2-SHA256, 310,000 rounds) using AES-256-GCM, and stored in this browser's local storage together with your compartment labels and your own fill history. Nothing in the stored record is readable without the passphrase.

The recovery key is the seed itself, written as camo- followed by 64 hex characters. Entering it on any device rebuilds every compartment address. Lock removes the unlocked key from the tab. Closing the tab does the same.

Compartments

Compartment n uses the secp256k1 private key HMAC-SHA256(seed, "camo/compartment/n"), and its address is the usual last 20 bytes of the Keccak-256 hash of the public key. Compartment 0 is Reserve, the wallet you fund and the one that pays for isolated buys. Every other compartment holds one position.

Key exports a compartment's private key as a standard 0x hex string that Robinhood Wallet, MetaMask and Rabby import.

Camo route

The route is NEAR Intents, reached through its 1Click interface. A quote returns a one-time deposit address. Once the deposit arrives, a solver pays the destination wallet in ETH on Robinhood Chain from the solver's own wallet. If the route cannot fill within its deadline, the deposit goes back to the refund address you gave.

Two uses: funding from any supported chain and asset, and internal moves of ETH between your own compartments (Reserve to a new compartment on an isolated buy, and back again with Return to Reserve). An internal move usually completes in under a minute. Every route transfer is listed under Activity with its live status.

Orders

Swap quotes come from Relay, which searches the pools on Robinhood Chain and returns the exact transactions for the trade. A buy is one transaction from the compartment. A sell is two: an approval for the token, then the swap. Each one is an EIP-1559 transaction for chain id 4663, signed in your tab and passed to the chain through the CAMO relay.

Robinhood Chain is an Arbitrum Orbit chain. Transactions go to one sequencer that orders them as they arrive, and there is no public mempool, so other traders cannot see an order before it is in a block.

Scatter

Scatter splits one buy into 2 to 6 slices of random size and sends them at random moments inside a time window, each priced and signed separately. The tab must stay open while a scattered order runs; the order card shows each slice.

Relay

The relay is the only server your browser contacts. It forwards market data, quotes, token pictures, balance lookups and signed transactions. It has no database, keeps no accounts, sets no cookies and writes no request log. The page's content policy (connect-src 'self', script-src 'self') stops the page from contacting anything else, and referrer-policy: no-referrer keeps outgoing links from carrying where you came from.

Privacy model

Hidden: which wallets belong to the same person; where a compartment's money came from; your address and viewing habits from data providers; your order from other traders until it is in a block.

Public, as on any chain: each compartment's own trades and balance. A determined observer can still compare amounts and timing across the route, which is why funding from another chain, round-number avoidance and Scatter exist.

Your part: keep the recovery key offline, do not send directly between compartments from an outside wallet, and do not reuse a compartment for a second token if you want the two positions kept apart.
