CAMO gives every position its own wallet and hides the thread between them, so the chain sees trades and never the trader.

What makes it private

Four layers between you and the watchers.

01

Compartments

Each token you buy lives in a fresh wallet, derived in your browser from a single seed. One position being found tells nobody about the next.

02

The camo route

Compartments are paid by a cross-chain route from its own wallet. No explorer shows your Reserve, or your main wallet, paying them.

03

No public mempool

Robinhood Chain orders go straight to the sequencer. There is no public queue where a bot can see your order and step in front of it.

04

Scatter

Split a buy into random slices over a time window, so size and timing stop looking like one trader.

The relay

Your browser speaks to one server. It remembers nothing.

Charts, quotes, token pictures and transactions all pass through the CAMO relay, so data providers and RPC nodes never see your address or what you are watching. There are no accounts, no cookies and no request log, and the page is locked by policy from loading anything off-site.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

What is CAMO?

A trading terminal for Robinhood Chain tokens built around privacy. It has what a terminal needs (trending, new pairs, live charts, a trade tape, one-click buys and sells) and adds a vault of compartment wallets, a private funding route and scattered orders.

Who holds my keys?

You do. A 32-byte seed is generated in your tab, encrypted with your passphrase (PBKDF2 with 310,000 rounds, then AES-256-GCM) and stored only in your browser. Every compartment key is derived from it. Transactions are signed in the tab (secp256k1, EIP-1559) and reach the relay already signed. The recovery key rebuilds every compartment on any device, and each compartment's private key can be exported to Robinhood Wallet, MetaMask or any EVM wallet.

How does the camo route break the link between wallets?

The route is NEAR Intents. You deposit to a one-time address, on Robinhood Chain or on another chain, and a solver pays the destination wallet in ETH from the solver's own wallet. On Robinhood Chain the compartment's history starts with a payment from the route, not from you. Moving ETH between two of your own compartments works the same way.

What can still be seen?

Everything a compartment does on Robinhood Chain is public, as with any wallet. What is missing is the thread from one compartment to another and to the wallet that funded them. Amounts and timing can still be compared by a determined observer, which is what Scatter and funding from another chain are for. A direct deposit to a compartment address is visible as a payment from the sender; the terminal labels those compartments so you always know which is which.

Why is there no sandwich protection switch?

Because the chain does not need one. Robinhood Chain is an Arbitrum Orbit chain: transactions go to a single sequencer that orders them as they arrive, and there is no public mempool for a bot to watch. Your signed order is sent to the chain's RPC and is not visible to other traders until it is in a block.

How are trades routed?

Quotes and swap transactions come from Relay, which finds the path across the pools on Robinhood Chain and returns the exact transactions to sign. A buy is one transaction. A sell is two: a token approval, then the swap. Your compartment signs each one in the tab.

Which chains can I fund from?

Any chain the route supports, including Robinhood Chain, Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Solana, BNB Chain, Bitcoin, Zcash, NEAR, Tron and more, in ETH, USDC, USDT, USDG, SOL, BTC and other majors. You always receive ETH on Robinhood Chain in the compartment you chose.

What does the relay know about me?

Nothing it keeps. It has no accounts and sets no cookies. It writes no request log. Rate limiting uses a salted hash that is thrown away every minute and re-salted on every restart.

What does it cost?

Network fees on Robinhood Chain, which are a fraction of a cent per transaction, the swap route's own fee shown in each quote, and the funding route's spread on transfers. Each compartment keeps 0.00015 ETH back to pay its own fees.

What are the risks?

Tokens are volatile and many go to zero. If you lose both your passphrase and your recovery key, nobody can recover the vault. Clearing your browser data removes the vault from that browser, so keep the recovery key somewhere safe before you deposit.
